# PDE5 inhibitor reporting comparison: post hoc extension

Frozen before new three-drug comparison results, 2026-10-13 (Asia/Shanghai). The investigators have already seen the tadalafil source-dependence audit. This is an explicitly retrospective, outcome-aware extension, not preregistration.

1. Reuse the original read-only eligible FAERS cohort, 2013Q1–2025Q4, selected by CASEVERSION then FDA_DT/PRIMARYID/quarter, excluding the contemporaneous deletion lists. Join new drug flags by selected PRIMARYID and quarter, never CASEID alone. All four cells use the same eligible background.
2. Primary exposure mapping is the exact generic word in DRUGNAME or exact PROD_AI ingredient token (explicit common salt forms admitted). Avoid substring analogues such as CHLOROPRETADALAFIL. Split active-ingredient mixtures by semicolon, slash, backslash, comma or plus, without guessing misspellings. Exact brand-name sensitivity: tadalafil CIALIS/ADCIRCA; sildenafil VIAGRA/REVATIO; vardenafil LEVITRA/STAXYN. Exclude rows coded DN (not administered); preserve mapping and conflicting-field audits. These are specified mappings, not an exhaustive validated ontology.
3. Primary comparisons: exact CSCR PT and PT containing RETINAL DETACHMENT; serous retinal detachment exact PT is a separately labelled sparse secondary endpoint. Outcomes can overlap, and broad RD is not synonymous with exudative RD. Count reports, not DRUG/REAC rows.
4. PS (primary suspect) is the primary role definition; all administered/unspecified roles are a sensitivity. This role choice was fixed before calculating the new comparison results. Compute ROR, log-Wald intervals, PRR with its own standard error, and BCPNN IC using the existing audited Dirichlet implementation (1,000,000 Monte Carlo draws, seed 20261013+row). IC intervals are 95% posterior credible intervals, not confidence intervals. Conditional exact OR intervals and Fisher two-sided P are retained for sparse tables. A zero cell triggers an explicitly labelled 0.5-all-cells Wald diagnostic, never silent replacement of the observed counts; a zero margin is nonestimable.
5. Compare source concentration among each drug/endpoint's reports by reporter country, receipt year/quarter, occupation, nonempty literature reference, and full authority identifier. Missing sources remain missing; identical source does not establish identical patients. Report counts, top-source share and HHI. Do not infer a drug difference merely from unequal RORs.
6. Full-background sensitivity excluding reporter-country SA/DE is fixed from the prior audit and applied symmetrically to every drug and every cell. Also perform whole-background leave-one-reporter-country-out for every country contributing >=2 exposed endpoint reports, retaining all such scenarios. These are geographic influence analyses, not duplicate corrections.
7. Active comparator: pairwise three-drug reporting odds within reports containing exactly one mapped study drug, excluding multi-drug co-reports. Pooling sildenafil and vardenafil is a separate comparator. Report unadjusted odds ratios and country-by-receipt-year Mantel–Haenszel estimates where informative strata exist. Age, dose, treatment duration and indication residual confounding remain; this is not comparative clinical effectiveness or proof of a class effect. Report multi-drug co-report counts.
8. Preserve every result, including no-event or unstable rows. No positive signal criterion establishes mechanism. Existing tadalafil cross-CASEID links will not be silently projected onto sildenafil/vardenafil; their patient independence remains unresolved.
